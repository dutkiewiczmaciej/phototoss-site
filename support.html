Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos deleted immediately?

No. Photos marked for deletion go to your "Recently Deleted" album first. They stay there for 30 days before being permanently removed. You can recover them anytime within that period.

Does PhotoToss upload my photos anywhere?

Absolutely not. All photos stay on your device. PhotoToss never uploads, shares, or transmits your photos over the internet. Your privacy is 100% protected.

How does the photo buffer work?

PhotoToss preloads photos so you can swipe without waiting. You can adjust the buffer size (50-1000 photos) in Settings. Larger buffers are great for airplane mode but use more memory.

Why do some photos take longer to load?

If you use iCloud Photo Library, some photos may be stored in the cloud. PhotoToss downloads them when needed. The cloud icon indicates when this is happening.

Can I review photos I've already seen?

Yes! Go to Settings and tap "Reset Review History". This clears your reviewed photos list and lets you start fresh.

What does "Swipe Up to Favorite" do?

Swiping up adds the photo to your Favorites album in the Photos app. It's a quick way to mark photos you love while reviewing.

How do I undo a swipe?

Tap the undo button in the bottom bar to go back to the previous photo. You can undo multiple swipes in a row.
