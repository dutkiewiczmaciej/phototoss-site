Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos deleted immediately?

No. Photos go to "Recently Deleted" and stay there for 30 days before permanent removal.

Does PhotoToss upload my photos?

Never. All photos stay on your device. PhotoToss works 100% offline.

How does the photo buffer work?

PhotoToss preloads photos for smooth swiping. Adjust buffer size (50-1000) in Settings.

Why do some photos load slowly?

iCloud photos download from the cloud when needed. The cloud icon shows this.

Can I review photos again?

Yes! Use "Reset Review History" in Settings to start fresh.

How do I undo?

Tap the undo button to go back. You can undo multiple times.
