PhotoToss

Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

PhotoToss is designed with your privacy as a top priority. We believe your photos are personal, and they should stay that way.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. PhotoToss operates entirely on your device.

  • Your photos are never uploaded to any server
  • We do not track your usage or behavior
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We do not require account registration

Photo Access

PhotoToss requires access to your photo library to function. This access is used solely to:

  • Display your photos for review
  • Delete photos you choose to remove (with your confirmation)
  • Add photos to your Favorites when you swipe up

All photo processing happens locally on your device. Photos are never transmitted over the internet.

Data Storage

PhotoToss stores minimal data on your device:

  • IDs of photos you've already reviewed (to avoid showing them again)
  • Your app preferences (like buffer size setting)
  • Lifetime statistics (photos deleted, space saved)

This data is stored locally using Apple's secure storage mechanisms and is never shared.

iCloud

If you use iCloud Photo Library, PhotoToss can access photos stored in iCloud. These photos are downloaded directly from Apple's iCloud service to your device - PhotoToss never acts as an intermediary.

Third-Party Services

PhotoToss does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

PhotoToss does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is intended for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
