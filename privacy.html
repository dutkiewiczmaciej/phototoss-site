PhotoToss

Privacy Policy

🔒 Your photos never leave your device. PhotoToss works 100% offline.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. PhotoToss operates entirely on your device.

  • Your photos are never uploaded to any server
  • We do not track your usage or behavior
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We do not require account registration

Photo Access

PhotoToss requires access to your photo library to function. This access is used solely to:

  • Display your photos for review
  • Delete photos you choose to remove (with your confirmation)
  • Add photos to your Favorites when you swipe up

All photo processing happens locally on your device.

Data Storage

PhotoToss stores minimal data on your device:

  • IDs of photos you've already reviewed
  • Your app preferences
  • Session statistics

This data is stored locally and never shared.

iCloud

If you use iCloud Photo Library, PhotoToss downloads photos directly from Apple's iCloud service to your device.

Third-Party Services

PhotoToss does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics, or advertising.
